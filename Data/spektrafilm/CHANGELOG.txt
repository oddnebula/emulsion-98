Emulsion 98 — changes to the spektrafilm profiles

Source: https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm (src/spektrafilm/data/profiles),
author Andrea Volpato, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (see SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt).
The profiles in ./profiles are unmodified copies downloaded 2026-11-10.

Derived file Sources/FilmEngine/MeasuredData.swift (generated by scripts/convert_spektrafilm.py)
contains a modified encoding of the same data, also CC BY-SA 4.0:
  - spectral data resampled to 380–780 nm in 5 nm steps (the profiles' native grid; linear interpolation where needed);
  - log10 spectral sensitivity converted to linear sensitivity;
  - missing spectral samples held at the nearest measured value; negative (masking) dye values kept;
  - characteristic and sublayer (fast / medium / slow emulsion) curves resampled to log exposure −3…+4 in 0.05 steps.
